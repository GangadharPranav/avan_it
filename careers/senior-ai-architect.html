Senior AI Architect

We are seeking a visionary AI Architect to design and implement robust generative AI pipelines, multi-agent frameworks, and vector search infrastructure for enterprise production environments.

Role Overview

As a Senior AI Architect at AVAN, you will lead the architecture and integration of advanced machine learning models and large language models (LLMs) into client codebases. You will design retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) structures, fine-tune models on proprietary domain data, and govern LLM orchestrators like LangChain or LlamaIndex.

Key Responsibilities

  • Architect, deploy, and scale multi-agent LLM systems for complex customer support and analytics automation.
  • Build high-performance semantic search pipelines using vector databases such as Pinecone, Qdrant, or Milvus.
  • Implement model evaluations, guardrails, and caching configurations to optimize runtime latency and token usage.
  • Collaborate with backend engineers to expose model inference endpoints securely via REST and gRPC API layers.
  • Conduct model training and fine-tuning experiments using PyTorch and Hugging Face pipelines.

Technical Requirements

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience, with 2+ years specialized in production AI/ML applications.
  • Demonstrated proficiency in Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face transformers, and LLM application frameworks.
  • Hands-on experience with vector database index design, chunking policies, and metadata filtering.
  • Familiarity with containerized deployments (Docker, Kubernetes) and cloud services (AWS SageMaker, GCP Vertex AI).
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Mathematics, or a related field.
