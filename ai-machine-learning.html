4-Step Vision Picking Technical Flow

Step 1: AR Display

Task Dispatch & HUD

SAP EWM dispatches picking orders directly to operator's smart glass heads-up display with aisle navigation arrows.

Step 2: Optical Scan

Computer Vision OCR

On-device camera scans bin locations and product barcodes without requiring handheld physical scanners.

Step 3: AI Validation

AI model verifies object dimensions and SKU batch number, alerting operator visually if an incorrect item is grabbed.
